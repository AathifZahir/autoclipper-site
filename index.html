Clip the hype moments from live streams, then post them in minutes

AutoClipper is a free, open-source Windows app for clip channels. It notices when Twitch chat explodes, clips the moment, turns it into a vertical video with a title and captions, and helps you post it to YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

How it works

  1. Pick what to watch. Choose a game (AutoClipper follows its biggest live streams) or a list of streamers.
  2. It finds the moments. When chat suddenly gets several times faster than normal, or several people type "clip it", AutoClipper asks Twitch to create a clip of the last 30–60 seconds.
  3. It makes a vertical video. The clip becomes a 1080×1920 video with a title at the top and word-by-word captions of what the streamer said.
  4. You review and post. In AutoClipper's control center you watch each video, let Gemini suggest a title and caption, edit them, and choose where to post.

One control center

Start and stop clipping, see what it's doing live, change settings and post clips, all from one page in your browser.

Moment detection

Reads public Twitch chat and spots spikes. One spammer can't trigger it, and you set limits like "6 clips per hour".

Skips betting streams

Leaves out gambling and betting streams automatically, and asks you about reruns, poker and streams that look like they have fake viewers.

Captions and titles

Speech-to-text runs on your PC for free. Optional Gemini titles from your own free API key.

YouTube Shorts

Upload to your channel as Public, Unlisted or Private, with title, description and tags.

TikTok

Send a video to your TikTok inbox to finish in the app, or post it from AutoClipper's TikTok posting screen.

Posting to TikTok

Connect your TikTok account with TikTok's own login page, then pick how to post each video:

  • Send to inbox: the video goes to your TikTok inbox as a draft. Open TikTok, add sounds or effects, and post.
  • Post to TikTok: a posting screen shows the video, which account it goes to, an editable title, who can view it (you choose, nothing is preselected), comment/Duet/Stitch settings and content disclosure. Nothing is posted until you click Post, and the screen then shows the post's status.

AutoClipper only acts on the TikTok account you connect, and only when you click. It never comments, likes, follows or messages.

Your data stays with you

AutoClipper runs on your computer. Your videos, settings and logins are saved there, not on our servers. The only online piece is a small login helper that finishes TikTok logins without storing anything. Read the Privacy Policy.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. AutoClipper is free and open source. Gemini titles use your own free Google AI Studio key.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, Python 3.11 or newer, and a Twitch account. YouTube and TikTok are optional.

Is it allowed to repost streamers' clips?

Clip only streamers who are OK with it. Many run official clipping programs. Every caption credits the streamer, and you're responsible for what you post.

Where do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub.
