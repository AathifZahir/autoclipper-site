What it is

AutoClipper is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and nobody can sign up for it.

How it works

  1. It watches live Twitch streams and notices when chat suddenly gets excited.
  2. It creates a Twitch clip of that moment and turns it into a vertical video with a title and captions.
  3. The owner reviews each video on their own computer, edits the title and caption, and chooses where to post it.
  4. When the owner clicks Post, the video is uploaded to the owner's own YouTube channel or TikTok account, with the visibility and settings they picked.

Nothing is posted without the owner reviewing it and clicking Post. Every video credits the original streamer in its caption.

Platforms it uses

  • Twitch: reads public chat and creates clips.
  • TikTok: sends videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, or posts them with the settings the owner chose on the Post to TikTok screen.
  • YouTube: uploads videos to the owner's channel as Shorts.
  • Google Gemini: suggests titles and captions from the clip's text.
