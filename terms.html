AutoClipper is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and nobody can sign up for it.

What it does

AutoClipper makes short vertical videos from Twitch stream highlights. When the owner chooses, it uploads selected videos to the owner's own TikTok account, either as drafts in the TikTok inbox or as posts with the settings the owner picked, and to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Use of TikTok

  • AutoClipper only acts on the TikTok account that signed in and approved it.
  • Nothing is posted until the owner previews the video and clicks Post.
  • It does not comment, like, follow or message on anyone's behalf.
  • Use of TikTok through AutoClipper also follows TikTok's own Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Music Usage Confirmation.

Content

The owner is responsible for the videos they choose to post. Each video credits the original streamer in its caption.
