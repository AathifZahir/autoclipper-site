Download AutoClipper

Version 1.1.0 for Windows 10 and 11. Free and open source.

Download the latest release (.zip)

Install

  1. Install Python 3.11 or newer. On the first installer screen, tick Add python.exe to PATH.
  2. Download the zip above and unzip it to a folder, for example Documents\AutoClipper.
  3. Double-click setup.bat. It installs what AutoClipper needs (about 600 MB the first time) and asks a few questions:
    • Twitch: log in with the Twitch account that will create the clips.
    • What to watch: a game or a list of streamers.
    • Limits: clip length, clips per hour and how easily a moment counts.
    • Text: captions, title, and an optional Gemini key for AI titles.
  4. Double-click AutoClipper.bat. AutoClipper opens in your browser.

Use

Double-click AutoClipper.bat. Everything is on one page:

  • Clipper: start and stop clipping, and see what it's watching and doing right now.
  • Clips: review your videos, let Gemini write titles and captions, and post to YouTube and TikTok.
  • Settings: what to watch, clip limits, text on the video, and your accounts.

Stopping always finishes the clip in progress first, and clipping keeps going even if you close the page.

Connect YouTube and TikTok

Open AutoClipper.bat and go to Settings → Accounts.

  • TikTok: click Connect next to TikTok and approve AutoClipper on TikTok's page.
  • YouTube: follow the Connect YouTube steps on the page to make a free Google login file (about 5 minutes), then click Connect.

You can disconnect either one at any time with the Disconnect button.
