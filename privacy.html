Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

AutoClipper is a free, open-source desktop app. It runs on your own computer. We (the AutoClipper project) don't run accounts, servers that keep your data, ads or analytics. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your computer only

  • Your settings, such as which game or streamers to watch, limits, and API keys you choose to add (for example a Gemini key).
  • Login tokens for Twitch, TikTok and YouTube, so you don't have to sign in every time.
  • Your videos, plus their titles, captions, transcripts and posting status.
  • Your channel decisions: streamers you allowed or told AutoClipper never to clip.

We can't see any of this. It stays in the AutoClipper folder on your computer.

Data from TikTok

When you connect TikTok, AutoClipper asks for these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok display name, avatar and username. These are shown so you know which account a video will go to, and used to link to your posted videos.
  • video.upload: sends a video you chose to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • video.publish: posts a video you previewed on the Post to TikTok screen, with the title and settings you chose there.

AutoClipper keeps the post ID and status TikTok returns, so it can show whether a post is live. It doesn't read your followers, messages, comments, analytics or anyone else's data.

The TikTok login helper

TikTok needs a secret key to finish a login, and a secret can't be included in an app anyone can download. So when you connect TikTok, your computer sends the one-time login code to the AutoClipper login helper, a small program on Cloudflare. The helper adds the key, asks TikTok for your login, and sends it straight back to your computer. It does the same when your login is renewed every 24 hours, and when you click Disconnect, it asks TikTok to revoke access.

The helper does not store or log codes, tokens or any other data. Its source code is public. Cloudflare, which runs it, processes the requests under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Other services AutoClipper talks to

  • Twitch: AutoClipper reads public chat in the channels you choose, only in memory, to spot moments. It creates clips with your Twitch account and downloads them.
  • YouTube: uploads the videos you choose to your channel, using your own Google login.
  • Google Gemini (optional, with your own key): receives a clip's transcript, recent chat lines and stream title, and suggests a title and caption.
  • Telegram (optional): sends finished clips to your own Telegram bot.

Each of these services handles that data under its own privacy policy.

How long data is kept

Data on your computer stays until you delete it. TikTok logins last until you disconnect, and TikTok's access tokens expire after 24 hours and are renewed automatically. The login helper keeps nothing.

Deleting your data

  • Click Disconnect next to TikTok or YouTube in the publisher. This deletes the saved login, and for TikTok also revokes AutoClipper's access.
  • Delete a video from the AutoClipper folder to delete its title, caption and status. Delete the whole folder to remove everything.
  • You can also remove access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and in your Google Account (Security → Third-party connections).
  • Videos you've posted can be deleted in the TikTok or YouTube app.

Children

AutoClipper isn't meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age to use TikTok, YouTube or Twitch where you live.
