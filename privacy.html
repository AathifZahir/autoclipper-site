AutoClipper is used only by its owner, on their own computer. It does not collect data from other people.

What AutoClipper stores

  • Login tokens from TikTok and YouTube, so the owner can upload without signing in each time. They are stored only in a file on the owner's computer.
  • Basic profile info (the TikTok display name and username), used only to show which account is connected and to link to posted videos.
  • The owner's own video files and their titles and captions, stored on the owner's computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process data about other TikTok or YouTube users.
  • It does not sell or share any data, and it has no ads or analytics.
  • It has no server of its own. Data goes only between the owner's computer and TikTok, YouTube, Twitch and Google Gemini (which suggests titles from clip text the owner chooses to send).

Removing access

The owner can disconnect at any time with the Disconnect button in AutoClipper, which deletes the stored login. Access can also be removed in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and in the Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
